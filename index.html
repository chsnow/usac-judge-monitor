Judge Monitor

A read-only alerting dashboard for USA Climbing judging on usac.results.info. It flags scores that were entered but not confirmed, and judge positions that have gone inactive. It never enters or changes scores.

Install (once)

On a laptop in Chrome or Edge, show the bookmarks bar, then drag this button onto it:

Judge Monitor

The bookmark always loads the latest version, so you never need to reinstall.

Use (at each comp)

  1. Log in to usac.results.info with an account that has admin access to the event.
  2. Open the results page of any category in the round being judged.
  3. Click the Judge Monitor bookmark and keep that tab open.

What the alerts mean

  • Not confirmed (red): a score is still unconfirmed, and the athlete has since been scored on a different boulder.
  • ERROR: Prior score changed (red): a confirmed result was changed in a way a new attempt can't explain: a top, zone or low zone removed or moved to another attempt, a result added on an earlier attempt, or the attempt count going down. Shows the old and new results. Stays until dismissed, even if it is put back. The monitor saves what it has seen for each round in your browser, so reloading the page or clicking the bookmark again picks up where it left off.
  • Not confirmed (yellow): a score has been unconfirmed longer than the limit (8 minutes by default, adjustable in the top bar).
  • Inactive judge position (yellow): a position with climbers still waiting has had no scoring activity for the limit (10 minutes by default, adjustable) while other positions are active. Often means the judge is logged into the wrong position.
  • Counts don't add up (yellow): for a climb, Total (registered in the category) doesn't equal Not started + Remaining + Done + DNS. Usually means a climber is missing from, or duplicated on, that boulder.
  • JP finished (green, no beep): every climber has topped every boulder at that position (DNS excluded) and no unstarted category uses it, so its volunteers can be released. Finished climbs and positions are greyed out in the table.
  • Everyone topped (blue, no beep): every climber in a category, except those marked DNS, has topped that boulder.

Only categories that are currently climbing are watched: categories that have not started yet, and categories whose results are finalized, are left out automatically.

Alerts on phones (optional)

The monitor can push red and yellow alerts to the phones of people on the floor, using the free ntfy app (no accounts). Messages lead with the judge position and give bib and category, never athlete names.

  1. In the monitor's top bar, click Phones, then Phone setup QR codes. Show or print that page for the crew: it has QR codes to install ntfy (iPhone and Android) and to subscribe to this comp's topic.
  2. Tick Send alerts from this laptop to phones on one laptop only, and click Send test to check phones get it.

The monitor laptop does the sending, so keep it plugged in, lid open and awake. You can lock the screen (Control-Command-Q on a Mac), but stop the laptop sleeping: on a Mac, open Terminal and run caffeinate -is and leave that window open (Control-C ends it). Anyone who knows the topic can read the alerts, so share it only with the judging crew.
